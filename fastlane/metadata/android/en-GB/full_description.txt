Door Lock Check is a simple, standalone Wear OS app that tracks whether you've
locked and checked your front door - so you never have to wonder while you're
out.

FEATURES

- Status tracking: a single button on your watch to mark the door as checked
  (locked) or unchecked (unlocked).
- Watch face complication: add the Door Lock Check complication to any
  compatible watch face to see your door status at a glance, with a matching
  lock icon.
- Instant, reliable state: your watch face and the app always agree, and
  startup never flashes the wrong status.
- Standalone: runs entirely on your watch. No phone app required, no account,
  no internet connection, no ads.

Door Lock Check does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your
door status stays on your watch.
